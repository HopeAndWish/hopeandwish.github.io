Seekmist Support

About / 关于应用

Seekmist is a nature sounds and ambient audio application designed to help users relax, focus, meditate, and sleep better.

寻谧音集（Seekmist）是一款自然环境音与氛围音应用，帮助用户放松身心、提升专注力、进行冥想以及改善睡眠体验。
